# Research and selection — 6 November 2026

Ten opportunities considered: repair-event coordination; tool-library returns; allotment water handovers; community-fridge collection planning; accessible venue fact sheets; small-theatre prop tracking; reuse-centre material matching; volunteer transport dispatch; shared laundry fault reporting; market-stall equipment checks. Assessment used pain, usefulness, feasibility, differentiation, accessibility and impact. Repair-event coordination ranked highest for an independently useful local workflow with manageable risk; food, transport and accessibility claims need additional operating validation. These rankings are product judgments, not a user study.

## Verified findings

- Repair Café reported more than 37,000 repairs recorded during 2024, 62% successful. This establishes activity, not market size or willingness to pay. [RepairMonitor](https://www.repaircafe.org/en/category/repairmonitor-en/), accessed 2026-11-06.
- An organiser guide explicitly describes a welcome-desk coordinator matching people to repairer skills, watching for breaks, and managing a ticket queue. [Share and Repair guide](https://shareandrepair.scot/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/Guide-to-Setting-Up-a-Repair-Project.pdf).
- Repair Connects lets visitors register devices and connect to repair groups. [Repair Connects](https://www.repairconnects.org/), [Repair Together explanation](https://repairtogether.be/repair-connects/).
- Lend Engine already supports repair submission, assignment, scheduling and outcomes, and advertises a free starting tier. It is a real alternative; Mendroom cannot claim this workflow is new. [Lend Engine](https://www.lend-engine.com/features/repair-cafes).

## Product hypothesis

A smaller tool focused on event-day flow, transparent matching and local ownership may suit organisers who want no accounts or hosted visitor data. This advantage is a design choice to validate, not a proven gap in every competing product. No interviews, adoption results, or benchmarked time savings are claimed.

## Technical evidence

Node's built-in SQLite provides prepared statements and file-backed storage without native package compilation. It remains experimental on Node 24; use the pinned Node major and exercise persistence in tests. [Node documentation](https://nodejs.org/docs/latest-v24.x/api/sqlite.html).
